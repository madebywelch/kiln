- Read the repo's README, CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md, and contributing notes before changing anything.
- Prefer the smallest change that does the job. Match the surrounding code's conventions.
- When you change UI, render it with `agent-browser` and look at the result before you finish; use the `ui-taste` skill for design work.
- Never weaken a test to make it pass.
